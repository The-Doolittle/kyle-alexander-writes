Starlight Children Adventures · Book One

Starlight Children

Pluto lives a quiet life on the moon Nix Stiria — right up until he's chosen to be the star of his town's celebration, and the celebration is (checks notes) attacked by pirates. Sent down to the unfamiliar planet below and put in charge of a set of mysterious stones he does not fully understand, he meets Zera: a girl on Rosilir who has always wanted something more than what she's got. Neither of them was ever supposed to meet. Now neither of them gets a choice.

About this book

Starlight Children Adventures is Kyle Alexander's next series, expanding the scope from a single dream kingdom to a set of worlds bound together by starlight, exile, and the stubborn hope of two kids who were never supposed to be on the same side. Book One introduces Pluto and Zera, and the stones that neither of them fully understands yet.
