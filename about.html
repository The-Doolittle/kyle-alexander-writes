About

The story behind the story.

Kyle Alexander writes from northwestern Pennsylvania — building worlds meant to be read aloud, argued over, and remembered.

I'm Kyle Alexander, and I love to write. I'm from northwestern Pennsylvania, and I write books that are full of hope, light, and imagination — the kind of stories that hold up whether you're eight or eighty. When I'm not writing, I'm usually spending time with my wife and son, or working away at whatever comes next in the cracks of time I can find.

"In the summer of 2016, my brother came up with a premise for a story. Falling in love with the idea, I devoted my time to writing it. In early 2020, he, unfortunately, passed away. I finished the book to prove that I could, and as a love letter to him."

That book became Kingdom of Dreams — my first independently published novel, co-credited to my brother Alexander, who dreamed it up in the first place. Finishing it was never really a question. It was the only way I knew to keep going, and to keep him in it.
