My story

The (abridged) story of me.

I'm from northwestern Pennsylvania, I write full-time now (or at least I'm trying to make "full-time" a real sentence and not just a goal), and most of what I write is meant to be read out loud — the kind of book you end up doing voices for.

Before any of it was books, it was just my family trading ideas back and forth — someone would throw out a "what if," and someone else would run with it. Writing, honestly, is just me continuing that habit by myself, badly, at 11pm.

Okay, here's the part that isn't a joke.

"In the summer of 2016, my brother came up with a premise for a story. Falling in love with the idea, I devoted my time to writing it. In early 2020, he passed away. I finished the book to prove that I could, and as a love letter to him."

That book is Kingdom of Dreams. It's co-credited to my brother Alexander, because it was his idea before it was mine, and finishing it stopped being optional the day he died. Everything I write now, I write a little bit for both of us.

Anyway. Thanks for reading this far — most people bail after the pirates. There's more books coming, and a newsletter if you want to know when.
